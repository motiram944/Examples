Our Origin

From Fashion Editorial To Global Weddings

Viyafilms emerged from a deep passion for preserving love in its purest form. Our journey began in fashion photography, where we developed visual precision and light sensitivity—skills that now bring every wedding film to life in the most authentic way.

Co-founders Brigesh, Vinay, and Nitin manage project execution and operations while Vinay coordinates our lineup of ~25 filmmakers. We deploy high-end full-frame Sony gear (A7IV, A7RIV, FX3) combined with external rental rigs for cinematic grandeur.
