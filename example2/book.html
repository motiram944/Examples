Frequently Asked Questions

What is your base wedding pricing?
Our wedding photography and cinematography packages start from ₹1,50,000 per day. Final pricing depends on the number of shoot days, travel logistics, and specific team size requirements.
How and when will we receive our photos and videos?
We deliver high-resolution color-graded photos via an online Pic-Time gallery within 30 days. Cinematic highlight films and full videos are delivered via our private ViyaFilms OTT streaming platform.
What is your payment structure?
We follow a structured 3-stage payment plan: 50% deposit upon booking to reserve the dates, 30% before or on the first day of the event, and 20% prior to final deliverable release.
Do you travel for destination weddings?
Yes! We have shot over 1,000 weddings in 4+ countries including Baku, Dubai, Kenya, Europe, and 20+ cities in India. We handle all logistics seamlessly.
