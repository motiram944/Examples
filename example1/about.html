Founding Vision

Vitthal Janardan — Founder & Creative Director

With co-founders Brigesh, Vinay, and Nitin leading project execution, our 25-member crew combines high-end Sony cinema cameras (ILCE-7M4, ILCE-7RM4) with drone cinematography to produce cinematic heirlooms.
